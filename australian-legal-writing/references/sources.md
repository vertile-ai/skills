## Limits

- Example citations are checked for citation details only; this skill relies on no passage of any case.
- AGLC4 appendices (report abbreviations, court identifiers) were not reproduced. Check uncommon abbreviations against the Guide.
- No free official citator covers every court. Treatment checks rely on AustLII and Jade noteup and are leads, not proof.
- Law changes. Re-check any provision cited here before relying on it after 22 November 2026.
